    Rebalancing

    Permitted moves under your thresholds

    Within a category

    Same asset, better venue: idle balances and laggards move to the best permitted venue of their own category.

    Between categories

    Mandate moves: what the policy asks for this tranche, taken from excluded venues, idle balances and the worst performers of one category, swapped, and deposited into the best venues of another. Same thresholds, caps and dilution ledger as the moves above; edit the amount to size it differently.

    Rates are shown after dilution. Supplying into a lending market spreads the same borrower interest over a bigger base, so a deposit earns less than the headline APY; each move is priced at r × TVL / (TVL + what this plan adds to that venue), several moves into one venue are counted together, and the drag on what we already hold there is subtracted from the pickup. The share threshold governs the resulting position, so what we already own in a venue is deducted from its room. Runs entirely in your browser against the stored snapshot. Sizing respects that threshold and the policy single-protocol cap where a policy exists. It does not model exit liquidity, gas, slippage or swap routes, and untracked positions are never moved. Execute hands a move to the local SafeAgent executor: it builds the Roles transaction, simulates it on Tenderly and, only after you approve in the popup, proposes it to the Safe. Nothing is signed or sent from this page.

    Order details▾

    Sell and buy lists are the CoW swap groups of this client's Roles permissions: any token in a group's sell set may be sold for any token in its buy set. TWAP groups are excluded. Tokens the bot's registry cannot build are greyed out.

    Quote, fee and slippage come from CoW through the bot (the same call the Telegram flow makes); slippage is CoW's dynamic tolerance for the pair and size. The quote refreshes every 30 s while the panel is open.

    Execute builds the approval and setPreSignature steps under Roles, simulates them on Tenderly and, after your approval in the popup, proposes the Safe transaction and submits the order to CoW. It fills asynchronously once solvers match it; nothing is signed on this page.
