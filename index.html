    Simulator

    Permitted moves under your thresholds

    Runs entirely in your browser against the stored snapshot. Sizing respects the venue TVL cap and the policy single-protocol cap where a policy exists. It does not model exit liquidity, gas, slippage or swap routes, and untracked positions are never moved. Execute hands a move to the local SafeAgent executor: it builds the Roles transaction, simulates it on Tenderly and, only after you approve in the popup, proposes it to the Safe. Nothing is signed or sent from this page.
